Fixed the trap-detection matcher two ways: it used a "length &lt;=" comparison that kept a trap flagged for the rest of the game once reached instead of only at the moment it applied, and it could only ever fire after a trap's full sequence (including the final capturing move) had already been played — meaning the "watch out" framing was always shown too late to matter. It now distinguishes the position right after the losing side's actual mistake (still informative, not still avoidable) from the position after the full sequence, with copy that matches which one it is.
